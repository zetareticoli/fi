About me

I'm a designer with
a love for coding.

For the past 17 years, I've worked as a Product Designer designing and building a wide range of digital products. I believe that good design should be both functional and aesthetically pleasing, and strive to create intuitive and engaging user experiences.

During my career, I've had the pleasure of working with a variety of clients in fields such as editorial, healthcare, banking, sports, public administration, e-commerce, and information technology. I worked for companies such as Scuderia Ferrari F1, Enel Energia, ING Direct, and HCL Software, as a freelance or full-time job. In 2009, I co-founded a SaaS company focused on domain management tools. The company and software was acquired in 2013.

Most recently, I serve as Senior Product Designer at the Italian Department for Digital Transformation where I'm responsible for designing and building public digital services for the Italian government.

I teach and speak about design systems, design tokens, and workflow best practices. I'm part of the faculty at POLI.design for the Executive Course on User Interface Design.

When not busy at work I love swimming, hiking in the Dolomites and traveling the world with my family. I'm passionate about astronomy and sci-fi movies.

I believe in a hands-on approach and working in cross-functional teams that include stakeholders as partners in the project.

I enjoy developing, learning, teaching, and working with colleagues who are honest, respectful, and provide direct feedback. Thorough research is essential in every project, as it helps understand the business and its users.
