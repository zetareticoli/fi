## From consultancy to leading the design team

Citynews gave me an incredible canvas for creativity and innovation. I started in 2012 as a freelance designer, redesigning the websites and community platforms for individual local editions.

My responsibilities expanded in 2015, when I was called back to spearhead a responsive design strategy and revamp the brand identity across all editorial products. In April 2016, I officially joined the company as **Head of Design**, going on to build and lead the product design team over the following years.

Throughout this journey, my work focused on three core products: a scalable shared brand identity, a cross-platform design system, and a refreshed corporate identity for the company itself.

### A brand identity built to scale

One of the earliest challenges was designing a **unique brand identity** that could be shared consistently across dozens of local and national editions, while remaining flexible enough to scale smoothly to new market openings as Citynews kept expanding.

### A design system for every platform

A significant milestone was the creation of the **Global Design Language (GDL)**, a design system built to let digital products be designed consistently across **web**, **iOS**, and **Android**. Introduced in late 2020, it's still in use today.

The GDL was built on Fractal, a tool that allowed us to create a styleguide that could be used by the design team and the development team.

### Native mobile apps

The design system also powered the evolution of the **iOS** and **Android mobile apps**, which I designed and helped develop from v1 to v6, managing external resources and suppliers along the way. I personally developed the responsive design strategy behind this work, optimizing the **design**, **front-end**, and **development** workflows.

### A corporate identity rooted in local storytelling

In 2018, we launched the new **corporate website**, built on a coherent, flexible, and scalable visual identity shared with all Citynews brands. Its main goal was to communicate the company's core value: a strong **presence across the territory** and the ability to tell the stories of local communities through **video services** and **reportage**.

### Internal tools

Alongside the editorial products, I directed the redesign of internal tools: 
- a **Content Management System** (CMS) built on proprietary software and used by 250+ editors 
- a **Customer Relationship Management**(powered by SuiteCRM) used by 100+ sales representatives


## Technical Details

- **Design System**: The Citynews Global Design Language (GDL) was crafted to ensure consistency across all corporate brands. It comprehended foundations (typography, color palette, and interaction guidelines), components, patterns and static pages.
- **Responsive Design Strategy**: Implementing a responsive design approach required reevaluating how design and development work together. 
- **Modern Front-End Workflow**: I helped develop a new front-end workflow, leveraging technologies such as Nunjucks, Git, Gulp, Node.js, and Fractal, to optimize collaboration between design and development.

## Personal Growth
My experience at Citynews was a **journey of personal growth**. 

I learned the value of **leadership**, **teamwork**, and **adaptability**. Collaborating with talented individuals and navigating the **dynamic landscape** of design and technology enriched my skillset. 

I enriched my project management skills and developed a keen eye for **detail**, crucial in maintaining design consistency and **quality**.
